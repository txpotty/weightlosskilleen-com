    What Weight Loss Maintenance Looks Like in Harker Heights, TX

    What Weight Loss Maintenance Means in Harker Heights

    Your body remembers its old weight. After a big loss, ghrelin tends to rise and resting energy use drops a little, so hunger comes back louder than it was before you started. A maintenance plan accounts for that instead of hoping it will not happen. For most GLP-1 patients that means a lower or spaced-out dose rather than an abrupt stop. Weigh-ins every week or two give your provider the trend line.

    Who Maintenance Care Is For

    Patients who join have usually lost at least 10 percent of their starting weight on a GLP-1 or through diet and exercise. Some hit goal and want less medication. Others already tried stopping on their own and saw the scale climb back up within a few months. Transfers count too. If you lost weight with another clinic, your provider continues from your current dose instead of starting over at the lowest one.

    Dosing and Tapering Schedules in Harker Heights

    Tapers move one dose level at a time. A common pattern for Wegovy is stepping from 2.4 mg to 1.7 mg, holding for four to eight weeks, and only then deciding whether another step makes sense. Tirzepatide steps down the same way, for example from 15 mg to 10 mg, then 7.5 mg if weight holds. Some patients settle on a steady lower dose and stay there for a year or more. Nothing changes until your provider reviews the last few weigh-ins.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Week one after a step-down feels normal. Semaglutide and tirzepatide both have half-lives close to a week, so appetite tends to return gradually across weeks two to four rather than overnight. A swing of one to three pounds in that window is common and often just water. At each check-in you rate your hunger from 1 to 10 so small changes show up on paper. A steady climb over three or four weigh-ins usually means pausing the taper.

    Side Effects on Lower Doses in Harker Heights

    Stomach effects ease on lower doses for most people. Some patients still get mild nausea or constipation the day after their shot. Rarely, GLP-1 medications are linked with gallbladder disease and pancreatitis, and fast weight loss itself raises gallstone risk, so severe upper belly pain that spreads to the back needs prompt in-person care. Rising hunger is expected as the dose comes down, and it is the main thing your provider watches. Report vomiting that lasts over a day.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening repeats at every check-in, not just once. People who are pregnant or planning pregnancy should not use GLP-1 medications, and neither should anyone with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN2. A past episode of pancreatitis or gallbladder surgery gets a closer look. Diabetes medications matter too, because insulin and sulfonylureas interact with GLP-1 dose changes. Patients who are not candidates get a plan built on protein targets and weekly weigh-ins instead.

    How Maintenance Compares to Active Treatment in Harker Heights

    Active treatment climbs. Maintenance looks for the floor. In the STEP 1 extension, people who stopped semaglutide 2.4 mg regained about two-thirds of the weight they had lost within a year, and in SURMOUNT-4 patients switched from tirzepatide to placebo regained roughly 14 percent of body weight while those who stayed on it kept losing. That is the main argument for a planned phase instead of quitting cold. Your provider looks for the lowest dose, or no dose, that keeps your weight inside a range you both agree on.
