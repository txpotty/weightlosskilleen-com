    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Belton, TX

    How Weight Loss Injections Work in Belton

    Most modern weight loss shots are GLP-1 medicines. They copy a hormone your gut releases after eating, which slows stomach emptying, signals fullness to the brain and helps regulate blood sugar after a meal. Semaglutide targets one receptor. Tirzepatide adds the GIP receptor, and studies have shown greater average weight loss with it.

    Who Weight Loss Injections Are For

    FDA-approved injections are for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. They work alongside diet and exercise. They are not for pregnancy or certain thyroid cancer histories.

    Injection Dosing Schedule in Belton

    Every weekly injection starts low. Wegovy starts at 0.25 mg and moves up every four weeks to 0.5, 1, 1.7 and finally 2.4 mg, with some patients holding at 1.7 mg. Zepbound begins at 2.5 mg and can go up by 2.5 mg at a time to a top of 15 mg. Same day each week.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Appetite often drops in the first week. The first month tends to show small changes on the scale since the dose is low, while stomach upset clusters right after each shot. In the STEP 1 trial, adults on semaglutide 2.4 mg lost about 15 percent of body weight on average over 68 weeks, and SURMOUNT-1 reported about 20 percent with tirzepatide 15 mg over 72 weeks. Results vary.

    Injection Side Effects in Belton

    Nausea is the classic one. Diarrhea, constipation, vomiting, heartburn and mild redness at the injection site are also common, especially in the days after starting or raising the dose. Eating slowly and skipping greasy food on shot day takes the edge off. Less common but serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones and kidney strain from dehydration.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Screening starts with BMI and medical history. Your provider then reviews every medication you take, looking hardest at insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills, since tirzepatide can make oral contraceptives less reliable after dose changes. A family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 is a hard stop. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are too.

    Brand-Name vs Compounded Injections in Belton

    Wegovy and Zepbound are FDA-approved for weight management. Ozempic and Mounjaro share those ingredients but carry type 2 diabetes labels instead. Compounded versions of semaglutide and tirzepatide come from licensed compounding pharmacies and lack FDA approval, which means no FDA review of their safety, effectiveness or manufacturing. Availability drives some people toward them.
