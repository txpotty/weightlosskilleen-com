    How Sildenafil Works in Killeen

    Sildenafil blocks PDE5. That enzyme normally breaks down cGMP, the molecule that relaxes smooth muscle in the blood vessels of the penis, so blocking it lets blood flow in more easily during arousal. Arousal is still required. The pill does not create desire or an erection out of nowhere. It makes it easier to get an erection and keep it firm enough for sex.

    Who Sildenafil Is For

    The usual candidate is an adult man with erectile dysfunction who has sex now and then and would rather take a pill only when needed. It works whether the cause is blood flow, nerves, stress or some mix. Diabetes, high cholesterol and treated high blood pressure are common in men who respond, and each deserves its own follow-up. Some men end up better suited to tadalafil. Mostly the ones who want a window longer than a few hours.

    Sildenafil Dosing and Timing in Killeen

    Take it by mouth 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Common strengths are 25 mg, 50 mg and 100 mg, and most men start at 50 mg. One dose per 24 hours, maximum. A greasy dinner can push the onset back by an hour, so a lighter meal beforehand helps. The effect usually lasts four to five hours.

    What the First Few Weeks Look Like

    Plenty of men notice a difference with the first dose. Others need several attempts before the timing clicks. A fair trial is four to eight separate attempts at a given dose, because first-time nerves and a couple of drinks can blunt the effect early on. Mild headache and flushing are common early. Jot down the dose, how long before sex you took it and how it went. That record is what your provider uses to adjust.

    Sildenafil Side Effects in Killeen

    Headache, facial flushing, a stuffy nose and indigestion are the most common side effects. Some men notice a blue tint to their vision or extra light sensitivity for a few hours. Dizziness is more likely with alcohol, with blood pressure medication and in men who stand up quickly after taking a 100 mg dose. Sudden vision loss, sudden hearing loss or an erection lasting more than four hours needs emergency care. Rare, but worth knowing.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule sildenafil out. Nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers are all nitrates, and pairing any of them with sildenafil can crash blood pressure. The pulmonary hypertension drug riociguat is excluded too. Questions cover recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain that comes on at rest and serious liver or kidney problems. One more question matters: can you climb two flights of stairs without chest pain?

    Sildenafil Compared to Other ED Options in Killeen

    Sildenafil kicks in within an hour and fades in about four to five hours. Tadalafil covers up to 36 hours, or can be taken every day at a low dose. Compounded chewables, rapid dissolve tablets and troches offer different formats, but those compounded versions are not FDA-approved. Generic sildenafil is FDA-approved and is chemically the same as Viagra, so it is often where providers start for men who want a short, predictable window. Short window, long track record.
