    How ED Troches Work in Georgetown

    ED troches usually contain sildenafil, tadalafil or both, which are PDE5 inhibitors. They relax the smooth muscle in penile blood vessels so blood flows in more easily when you are aroused, the same mechanism as standard ED tablets. You tuck it into your cheek. It dissolves slowly over 10 to 20 minutes, part absorbing through the cheek and part swallowed. Arousal is still required.

    Who ED Troches Are For

    Troches may suit men with ED who have trouble swallowing tablets or simply want a different format. A few men who did not respond fully to standard tablets try compounded strengths or combos, always with a provider watching blood pressure. They are not a daily treatment. Stronger is not automatic. Your provider decides if it fits.

    Troche Dosing and Use in Georgetown

    Take a troche as needed, usually 30 to 60 minutes before sex. Place it between your cheek and gum and let it dissolve slowly without chewing, which typically takes 10 to 20 minutes. Skip eating and drinking until it is gone. No more than one dose a day. Some troches are scored so you can use half.

    What to Expect in the First Weeks

    Most men notice an effect on the first or second use. Timing takes a few tries. Give it at least four to six separate attempts before judging, because first-time nerves, a few drinks or a heavy meal can weaken the early results. The flavored base takes some getting used to. Report back to your care team.

    ED Troche Side Effects in Georgetown

    Headache, flushing, nasal congestion, indigestion and dizziness are the common ones.     Eligibility and Screening

    Nitrates rule troches out, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide and poppers. So does riociguat. Recent heart attack or stroke, chest pain at rest, poorly controlled blood pressure and significant liver or kidney disease call for a close look. Alpha blockers and strong CYP3A4 inhibitors get flagged. Fitness for sex is asked about too.
