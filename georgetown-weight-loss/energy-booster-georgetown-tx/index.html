    The Energy Booster is a compounded formula built around nutrients involved in how the body makes and uses energy, especially B vitamins like B12.     Adults who feel tired most days despite seven or more hours of sleep, and who want a provider-guided approach rather than store-bought energy drinks, are the usual fit.
